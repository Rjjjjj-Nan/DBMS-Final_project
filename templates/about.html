About LOSTLINK

Building a community that cares about lost and found items

Our Mission

LOSTLINK is dedicated to helping people recover lost items and return found items to their rightful owners. We believe that every lost item has a story, and our mission is to reunite people with what matters most to them.

Our Vision

We envision a world where lost items are quickly recovered, where communities are more connected, and where people look out for one another. Through technology and human kindness, we're building a platform that brings people together.

Why We Started

LOSTLINK was created to address a real problem: the hassle and stress of losing important items. We wanted to create a centralized platform where people can report lost and found items, making it easier to reconnect belongings with their owners.
